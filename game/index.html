Chert’s mission

Mabel stole Chert’s spark stone and sealed it in a room upstairs. Without it, he can’t connect a single call.

  1. Search the furniture for Mabel’s brass key. Clues and ringing phones help.
  2. Climb the stairs and find the room with the red glowing door.
  3. Unlock it and strike the spark stone first to win.
Every turn, each player says one move: a door (left, right, forward, back), the stairs, or something to search. Then the moves play out. If Mabel finds you, do what she says or you’re disconnected.
